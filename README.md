## 🎀 **What is Enigma of Anime?**

**Enigma of Anime** is a cute, interactive project designed to explore hidden lore, character secrets, and the greatest mysteries across the anime multiverse. Whether you're decoding a mystery from a magical girl show or tracking down shounen easter eggs, everything is better in 3D! 🧊✨

> **Note:** Grab your favorite strawberry milk 🍓🥛 before you start exploring the codebase!

---

## 🌟 **Magical Features**

*   🔮 **Lore Decoder:** Decrypt hidden messages from your favorite shows.
*   🧸 **3D Character Viewer:** Inspect adorable 3D models of iconic anime items.
*   🌸 **Kawaii UI/UX:** A user interface so sweet it'll give you a toothache.
*   ✨ **Daily Anime Trivia:** Expand your brain power with daily enigmas.
